    Participation

    Who is taking part

    Individual ratings and survey answers are private, so they are not shown here; the pipeline uses them to compute combined results.

    Members

    Invitations

    Create a link and send it through your usual channels (email, intranet, a letter with a QR code). A link can be for one person or for a group, and expires after the days you choose.

    Fairness

    Groups and their share

    A change is endorsed only when every group finds it helpful. Set the groups and the share of the community each stands for (for example from staff lists and patient numbers). Each group then counts at that share, however many of its members take part.

    How groups are formed
    AI help Off by default. Concerns, scores and compromises never use AI.

    After the round

    Publish, train and report

    • Publish the new charter version
      Endorsed changes, and bridges endorsed in the bridge phase, become a fixed version with its dissent record. The Charter page shows it as tracked changes.      End of round
    • Confirm the test cases
      Each test case of an adopted change is confirmed by two members other than its author.      One week
    • Check the safety floor
      The new version must still pass every floor case. A change that breaks one is held back for review.      Pipeline
    • Split the test cases 80/20
      Fixed by each case's ID, so nobody can move a case between training and testing.      Pipeline
    • Fine-tune the model
      Model set in kit.config.json; the default is Apertus v1.1 1.5B, which fits a free cloud GPU.      Pipeline
    • Publish the scorecard
      Before and after, on held-out cases and the safety floor.      Pipeline
