Explainer

What is an AI charter?

What it is

A short text of guidelines for how an AI should behave. For example: be honest, avoid harm, respect people’s choices.

What it’s for

The AI is trained to follow these guidelines. They shape what the AI will help with, what it refuses, and how it talks to you, the users.

Why does phrasing matter?

Most people agree on principles like “avoid harm”. However, the exact phrasing can decide what that means in practice, which is where nuance and specificity are important.

Learn more: what a charter covers, and how it gets into the AI

What a charter covers

  • How to handle sensitive or risky questions
  • What the AI must never do, such as discriminate or spread false information
  • How open the AI is about its limits and decisions
  • When a human has to step in
  • What happens with personal data

Why it matters

A written charter makes the AI’s behaviour more predictable and easier to check, so people can trust it. In short: an AI should not end up with values that nobody consciously chose.

How it gets into the AI

  1. Training examplesExample questions and good answers are written based on the charter. The AI is trained on them, so it learns to act that way.
  2. Instructions in every chatSome providers also give the model its rules as hidden instructions (a “system prompt”) at the start of every conversation.
  3. Extra safety checksSometimes separate filters check questions or answers and step in when something breaks the rules.

For Apertus, the charter is used in training: as example answers the model learns from, and as the yardstick for scoring its answers.

No method is perfect: a model can still break its charter. That’s why we test the result.

From your suggested changes to a new model

  1. CollectYour ratings and suggested changesAll suggested changes are counted equally.
  2. DecideBroad supportA charter change passes when there is majority approval.
  3. TrainA new versionWe train a small copy of Apertus on the charter changes that passed.
  4. CheckA public scorecardWe test it on new questions and benchmarks, and publish whether it really changed behaviour.
