Your data

  • We only need your email to sign in. We never show it.
  • Age, language and AI use are optional, and only shown as totals for groups of 5 or more.
  • We never show who answered what.
  • Someone with several email addresses could answer twice. We accept that rather than ask for more data.

Why this kind of participation?

Most research on “pluralistic” AI looks at what a model says: does it present many points of view? We look at who gets to write the rules. Researchers call this institutional pluralism: people can take part in, contest and revise the rules an AI follows.

We are honest about the limits. Our results do not bind the official Apertus. On Arnstein’s “ladder of citizen participation”, that is still consultation, not real decision-making power. We want to show what the next step could look like.
