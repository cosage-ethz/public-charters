Changes most popular with smaller subgroups

Not every suggested change wins a majority. These changes are backed by most people in one "demographic" group, even though most people overall don’t agree (yet). We want them to stay visible, so smaller voices are not lost.

How we aggregated opinions
  • We classify a change as "Most people agree" if: clearly more yes than no, from at least 10 answers.
  • We classify a change as causing divided opinions if: at least 30% yes and at least 30% no.
  • Age, language and AI use is only used to analyse how different groups rate and propose changes, and is never used to decide approvals.
  • “Bridges groups”: a badge for changes that most people support in every age, language and AI-use group. It never decides on its own.
  • Rival wordings: if several wordings for the same words pass, the one with the most support is chosen. Close calls may go to a head-to-head vote later.

Note: this is a first version: we may adjust these classification limits once many real answers come in.
