The rule

A change needs broad support

  1. Everyone counts the same

    Each rating counts +1 if helpful, 0 if somewhat and −1 if not helpful. A suggestion’s net score is the average, from −1 to +1.

  2. Broad support, visible disagreement

    A suggestion is endorsed with a net score of at least +0.3 from at least 10 ratings. If at least 30% find it helpful and at least 30% do not, it is contested and goes to a bridge challenge.

  3. Breakdowns, not vetoes

    If people answer the optional questions on age, language and AI use, you can see how each group rated a change. Groups never block a change, and groups with fewer than 5 answers are not shown.

These thresholds are a first version and may change once real ratings come in. One person with several email addresses could vote more than once; with a small, open round we accept that risk and say so here.
