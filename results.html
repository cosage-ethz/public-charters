The rule

A change needs support in every group

  1. Groups count at their share

    Each group counts at its share of the community, set by the organisers, not by how many of its members turned up. A busy, well-organised group cannot outweigh the others.

  2. Every group has to agree

    A suggestion is endorsed when at least 60% of raters in every group find it helpful, with at least 3 ratings per group. If some groups are for it and some against, it is contested.

  3. Disagreement stays visible

    Each endorsed change carries a dissent record: which groups were partly against, and their reasons. Contested changes go to a bridge challenge, where members write wordings both sides can accept.

Weight = share of the community ÷ share of active members, kept between 0.3 and 3. This is a simplified version of DemPO weighting. Helpful shares are smoothed: each group starts with one imaginary helpful and one unhelpful rating, so two out of two does not count as 100%.
